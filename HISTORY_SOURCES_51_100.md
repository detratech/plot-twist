| 66 | Subprime mortgage boom · 2000s | [Federal Reserve History](https://www.federalreservehistory.org/essays/subprime-mortgage-crisis) and [Federal Reserve Bank of New York](https://www.newyorkfed.org/newsevents/speeches/2010/tra100625.html) | Many subprime mortgages used teaser-rate adjustable structures that looked manageable initially but exposed borrowers to payment shock and refinancing risk later. | 68 | Anna Sorokin’s fake-heiress years · 2010s | [TIME on Sorokin’s conviction and sentencing](https://time.com/5560432/anna-delvey-sorokin-trial/) | Sorokin persuaded banks, hotels and people around New York that she was a wealthy German heiress, using the appearance of wealth and social status to obtain real access before the deception collapsed. |
| 69 | MC Hammer bankruptcy · 1996 | [Washington Post contemporary report](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/lifestyle/1997/08/26/its-bankruptcy-time/3cc1bc3f-4065-42fa-802c-8ea7bfc3b1ee/) and [New Yorker profile](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/1996/08/26/under-the-hammer-2) | Hammer earned extraordinary sums, maintained a huge entourage/payroll and lavish lifestyle, then filed for bankruptcy as income fell and obligations remained. | 71 | Installment buying in the 1920s | [Library of Congress: 1927 installment-plan study](https://www.loc.gov/item/27008285/) and [Smithsonian: consumer credit](https://americanhistory.si.edu/explore/exhibitions/american-enterprise/online/corporate-era/shopping) | Installment purchasing and consumer credit expanded sharply in the 1920s. Productive debt can create both capacity and fragility. |
| 73 | Employee stock options and vesting | [Journal of Financial Economics: “The option to quit”](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X1730257X) | Broad-based employee stock options can reduce turnover before vesting, with turnover rising after the retention period. | 75 | Women Airforce Service Pilots · World War II | [U.S. Air Force history](https://www.af.mil/News/Features/Article/497548/womens-legacy-parallels-air-force-history/) and [National Women’s History Museum](https://www.womenshistory.org/exhibits/women-airforce-service-pilots-wasps-wwii) | More than a thousand women completed rigorous flight training and performed ferrying, test, training and other military aviation duties, demonstrating relevant capability despite earlier role exclusions. |
