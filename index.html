HOW TO PLAY

How to Play

  1. Pick a mode. Sugar Coated for Snowflakes is the current 100-card deck. Cutthroat Honest is being built next.
  2. Pick a topic, mix a few, or leave Mix Everything on.
  3. Read the card out loud.
  4. Pick one side before you see the twist. Both choices are meant to be reasonable.
  5. Say why you picked it. No “it depends” until you choose a side.
  6. Reveal the Plot Twist. You just learned something new, so changing your mind is completely fair.
  7. Read The Point, answer the next question, and check out the Real-World Example.
  8. One Last Thing directly answers that question after you have seen the example.
  9. Use Keep Talking if your group wants a couple more questions.
  10. Tap Chaos when you want to make the discussion harder or funnier.
There are no trick answers. Pick what you really think, explain it, then see whether the twist changes anything.
