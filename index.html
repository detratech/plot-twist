How to Play

  1. Start Game shuffles the whole deck. You should not know what kind of card is coming next.
  2. Read the scenario aloud.
  3. Everyone chooses an answer.
  4. No “it depends” until after choosing.
  5. Defend your answer.
  6. Reveal the Plot Twist.
  7. Change your mind if you want.
  8. Argue with the answer, not the person.
  9. Hit Chaos whenever you want to put the current answer under pressure or make someone defend it differently.
Some cards are ridiculous. Some are uncomfortable. Some are just good arguments. Do not assume every card is trying to teach the same thing.
