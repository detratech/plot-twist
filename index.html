HOW TO PLAY

How to Play

  1. Pick one or more topics on the home screen, or leave Mix Everything selected.
  2. Start Game shuffles only the topics you picked.
  3. Read the scenario aloud.
  4. Pick one of the answer bubbles before anyone reveals the twist.
  5. No “it depends” escape tunnel until after you commit.
  6. Defend your answer. Roasting the argument is allowed. Roasting the person is lazy.
  7. Reveal the Plot Twist.
  8. Read The Point. That is the principle the scenario was built to expose.
  9. Use Where This Can Go if the group wants to keep digging.
  10. Use Chaos when somebody is getting suspiciously comfortable with their own reasoning.
The scenarios are meant to be funny enough to argue about and sharp enough to follow you home. Pick first. Defend it. Then see what the twist does to your reasoning.
