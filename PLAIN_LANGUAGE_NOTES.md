# Plot Twist Plain-Language Standard

Player-facing writing should sound like normal people talking around a table.

## Default voice

- short, direct sentences
- familiar everyday words
- conversational questions
- one idea at a time
- humour that sounds natural when read aloud
- concrete examples before abstract labels

## Avoid

- academic or debate-club wording
- legalistic phrasing unless the joke truly needs it
- stacked metaphors in the same sentence
- jargon such as `epistemic`, `provenance`, `corroboration`, `falsifiability`, `normative`, or `methodology`
- sentences that make players reread them to understand the point
- clever wording that hides the actual choice

## Test

Before shipping a line, ask: **Would a regular person naturally say or understand this out loud on the first read?**

If not, rewrite it.

The goal is not to make the ideas shallow. The goal is to make the ideas easy to enter.
